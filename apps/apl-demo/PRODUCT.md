# Product — Kubri x APL (demo)

## Register

product (Operate). It is a clickable sales demo of a future product, so every screen must look
like real, trustworthy software, not like a pitch deck.

## Users

- **Who watches**: commercial and recruiting managers of Italian Employment Agencies (Agenzie per
  il Lavoro, APL). They are the buyers. A Kubri salesperson drives the demo on a laptop, screen
  shared or in a meeting room, in about 10 minutes.
- **Who the screens pretend to be**: an APL recruiter, and an operator of an association
  (social cooperative, reception centre, re-entry association) in the APL's local network.
- Companies that hire are **not** users: the APL presents candidates to them outside Kubri.

## Product purpose

Show how an APL activates a territorial network of associations with one action: publish a job
offer → Kubri matches it against the shared candidate database → compatible associations are
notified → each association reviews suggested candidates, adds others and approves who to send →
the APL receives full profiles and contacts.

Decisions confirmed by the owner (2026-11-17):

- The demo is sold to APLs; the "company" in Kubri's marketing is the APL itself.
- The APL sees candidates without name and contacts until an association sends them.
- The association approves who is sent; that approval is the gate.

## Brand

Kubri Società Benefit: warm, human, valuing; plain second-person Italian. Inherit the existing
Kubri world (dashboard primary violet, Poppins). No gamification, no slideware.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS card grids and hero-metric templates.
- A marketing page pretending to be an app.
- Anything that makes vulnerable candidates look like inventory: people keep names, stories and
  context; scores are explained, never bare verdicts.

## Assumptions (not yet confirmed)

- Italian only, laptop first (works down to tablet).
- One generic demo dataset ("Agenzia Demo", Turin area, logistics offer) rather than per-prospect data.
