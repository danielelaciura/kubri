# Piano 05 — i18n e simulatore da terminale

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Trasformare i `TextRef` prodotti dal motore in testi da inviare, nella lingua della
sessione (catalogo dei testi di sistema, traduzioni curate, cache, traduttore), e permettere di
fare un'intervista completa in terminale con `kubri-bot campaigns simulate <file>`, senza
database, senza WhatsApp e senza LLM.

**Architecture:** Tutto il modulo `src/i18n/` resta senza I/O diretto: il traduttore
(`Translator`) e la cache (`TranslationCache`) entrano come interfacce. In questo piano esistono
solo implementazioni finte o in memoria; il traduttore LLM arriva nel piano 06 (modulo `nlu`) e
la tabella `TranslationCache` nel piano 08 (modulo `store`). La risoluzione traduce sempre il
**modello** con i segnaposto (`{value}`), mai il messaggio già interpolato: i valori del
candidato non raggiungono mai il traduttore. Il simulatore sta in `src/cli/simulate/`: un ciclo
`step()` → `resolveSend()` sopra un'interfaccia `Interpreter` (definita qui in
`src/nlu/interpreter.ts`, implementata dall'LLM nel piano 06), con un interprete "naive"
deterministico e comandi `/…` che forzano qualunque interpretazione.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, Zod 4, commander 15, `node:crypto` (sha256), `node:readline`,
Vitest 4. Nessuna dipendenza nuova.

**Spec:** §5.6 (benvenuto e lingua), §7.1–7.3 (effetti e consenso), §8.1 (compiti di `nlu`, per
il contratto con il piano 06), §9 (multilingua), §10.3 (`TranslationCache`, `Consent`), §14
(test manuali con `simulate`), D13, D16, D23, D32, D33, D41, D49, D69.

## Decisioni prese in questo piano (da riportare nella spec, §2, come D70–D79)

- **D70 — Si traduce il modello, non il messaggio.** La traduzione riguarda il testo italiano
  con i segnaposto (`Per «{field}» ho capito: {value}`); i parametri si inseriscono dopo. Così i
  valori del candidato non vanno mai al traduttore (minimizzazione) e la cache vale per tutti i
  candidati. Le etichette dei campi sono a loro volta testi tradotti (tipo `label`).
- **D71 — Segnaposto.** Sintassi `{nome}` (lettera iniziale, poi lettere, cifre, `_` e `.`, per
  esempio `{dataController.name}`). Un segnaposto senza parametro resta visibile e viene
  riportato in `missingParams`, perché il runtime lo registri. Un valore inserito non viene
  riletto. Una traduzione che non conserva **esattamente** l'insieme dei segnaposto del testo
  italiano viene scartata.
- **D72 — Ripiego sull'italiano.** Se il traduttore fallisce, o la traduzione viene scartata, si
  invia il testo italiano (`source: "fallback"`, `language: "it"`), che non viene salvato in
  cache. Un errore della cache non blocca mai una risposta. Mai silenzio.
- **D73 — `isCurated`.** Vale `true` per un testo rivisto da una persona: l'originale italiano,
  una traduzione curata della campagna, una voce di cache con `reviewed = true`. Una traduzione
  LLM, appena fatta o in cache non rivista, vale `false`. È il valore che il runtime salva in
  `Consent.textShownIsCurated`.
- **D74 — Testi critici.** Sono critici i testi dei passaggi `consent`, inline o per `ref`. i18n li
  riconosce dalla campagna, senza cambiare `TextRef`. Se il testo mostrato non è curato, al
  messaggio si aggiunge una riga, tradotta anch'essa, con il link al testo ufficiale: il `link`
  del passaggio, altrimenti `dataController.privacyUrl`. `requireCurated` (§9) è rinviato.
- **D75 — Parametri tipizzati nel catalogo.** Ogni voce del catalogo dichiara i propri parametri
  con un tipo:
  - `text` e `number`: inseriti così come sono;
  - `field`: l'etichetta leggibile del campo (campi standard: `src/i18n/field-labels.ts`;
    personalizzati: `label` della campagna), mai il riferimento grezzo `custom.x`;
  - `value`: booleani → "sì"/"no", `null` → "(nessun valore)", elenchi → separati da virgole,
    date ISO → `gg/mm/aaaa`; gli altri valori restano come sono (normalizzati in italiano, §6);
  - `flag`: non si inserisce, sceglie solo una variante del testo
    (`system.post_completion_help`).
- **D76 — `purpose` di `SendText`.** `clarify` con il parametro `missing` diventa un unico
  messaggio, con la riga del catalogo `send.clarify` ("Mi serve ancora un dettaglio:
  {missing}") seguita dalla domanda. `rephrase` antepone `send.rephrase` alla domanda. La
  riformulazione vera da parte dell'LLM (§8.1 punto 3) è rinviata. `missing` è testo libero che
  l'NLU scrive **nella lingua della sessione** (vincolo per il piano 06) e si inserisce senza
  traduzione.
- **D77 — Lingua di risoluzione.** I testi si risolvono in `state.language ??
  campaign.defaultLanguage`, letta **dopo** `step()`: i testi di un turno che imposta la lingua
  escono già nella lingua nuova. Il benvenuto (lingua ancora `null`) esce quindi nella lingua
  di default della campagna e contiene già la riga in inglese (§5.6 punto 1).
- **D78 — Cache delle traduzioni.** Interfaccia `TranslationCache` in `i18n`, chiave (sha256 del
  testo italiano, lingua). In questo piano esiste solo l'implementazione in memoria; la tabella
  Prisma arriva nel piano 08 insieme al runtime che la usa.
- **D79 — Simulatore.** `campaigns simulate <file>` lavora sul file, senza database. Interprete
  `naive` (deterministico: sì/no, opzioni, valore della domanda corrente convertito nel tipo
  del campo) e comandi `/…` che forzano ogni interpretazione (`/choice`, `/edit`, `/low`,
  `/partial`, `/offtopic`, `/help`, `/refuse`, `/unclear`, `/null`, `/nlu`). I comandi valgono
  con qualunque interprete, anche con l'LLM del piano 06. Traduttore finto `[xx] …`. Interfaccia
  `Interpreter` in `src/nlu/interpreter.ts`.

```ts
/** `{name}` placeholders (D71): a letter, then letters, digits, `_` and `.`. */
const PLACEHOLDER = /\{([A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_.]*)\}/g;

/** The distinct placeholder names of a template, sorted. */
export function placeholdersOf(template: string): string[] {
  const names = [...template.matchAll(PLACEHOLDER)].map((match) => match[1] ?? "");
  return [...new Set(names)].sort();
}

/** True when both templates use exactly the same set of placeholders. */
export function samePlaceholders(a: string, b: string): boolean {
  const left = placeholdersOf(a);
  const right = placeholdersOf(b);
  return left.length === right.length && left.every((name, i) => name === right[i]);
}

export interface Interpolated {
  readonly text: string;
  /** Placeholders with no value: left visible, so runtime can log them. */
  readonly missing: readonly string[];
}

/** One pass: inserted values are never scanned again (a candidate may type "{value}"). */
export function interpolate(
  template: string,
  values: Readonly<Record<string, string>>,
): Interpolated {
  const missing = new Set<string>();
  const text = template.replace(PLACEHOLDER, (whole, name: string) => {
    const value = Object.hasOwn(values, name) ? values[name] : undefined;
    if (value !== undefined) return value;
    missing.add(name);
    return whole;
  });
  return { text, missing: [...missing].sort() };
}
```

```ts
import type { LanguageCode } from "./localized-text";

/** What a text is, so the translator can be stricter with consents (spec §9, D74). */
export type TranslationKind = "system" | "campaign" | "label" | "critical";

export interface TranslationRequest {
  /**
   * An Italian template: its `{placeholders}` must come back unchanged (D71). Never a
   * candidate's value: templates are translated before interpolation (D70).
   */
  readonly text: string;
  readonly sourceLanguage: LanguageCode;
  readonly targetLanguage: LanguageCode;
  readonly kind: TranslationKind;
}

export interface Translation {
  readonly text: string;
  /** Stored with the cached translation (spec §10.3). */
  readonly provider: string;
  readonly model: string;
}

/**
 * Translates Italian templates. nlu implements it with the LLM (plan 06). It throws when no
 * translation is available: i18n then falls back to Italian (D72).
 */
export interface Translator {
  translate(request: TranslationRequest): Promise<Translation>;
}
```

```ts
import { createHash } from "node:crypto";
import type { LanguageCode } from "./localized-text";

export interface CachedTranslation {
  readonly text: string;
  /** Checked by a person (spec §9): such a translation counts as curated (D73). */
  readonly reviewed: boolean;
  readonly provider: string;
  readonly model: string;
}

/** Spec §10.3, keyed by (sourceHash, language). The Prisma table comes with plan 08 (D78). */
export interface TranslationCache {
  get(sourceHash: string, language: LanguageCode): Promise<CachedTranslation | undefined>;
  set(sourceHash: string, language: LanguageCode, entry: CachedTranslation): Promise<void>;
}

/** sha256 of the Italian source text. */
export function sourceHash(text: string): string {
  return createHash("sha256").update(text, "utf8").digest("hex");
}

/** For tests and the simulator: lives as long as the process. */
export class MemoryTranslationCache implements TranslationCache {
  private readonly entries = new Map<string, CachedTranslation>();

  get(hash: string, language: LanguageCode): Promise<CachedTranslation | undefined> {
    return Promise.resolve(this.entries.get(`${hash}:${language}`));
  }

  set(hash: string, language: LanguageCode, entry: CachedTranslation): Promise<void> {
    this.entries.set(`${hash}:${language}`, entry);
    return Promise.resolve();
  }

  get size(): number {
    return this.entries.size;
  }
}
```

```ts
import { DEFAULT_LANGUAGE, type LanguageCode, type LocalizedText } from "./localized-text";
import { samePlaceholders } from "./template";
import {
  sourceHash,
  type CachedTranslation,
  type TranslationCache,
} from "./translation-cache";
import type { Translation, TranslationKind, Translator } from "./translator";

export type TemplateSource = "reference" | "curated" | "cache" | "translated" | "fallback";

export interface ResolvedTemplate {
  readonly template: string;
  /** The language the template is actually in: Italian after a fallback (D72). */
  readonly language: LanguageCode;
  readonly source: TemplateSource;
  /** Reviewed by a person (D73). */
  readonly isCurated: boolean;
}

export interface TemplateServices {
  readonly translator: Translator;
  readonly cache: TranslationCache;
}

/** Spec §9: curated translation → cache → translator; Italian when all else fails (D72). */
export async function resolveTemplate(
  text: LocalizedText,
  language: LanguageCode,
  kind: TranslationKind,
  services: TemplateServices,
): Promise<ResolvedTemplate> {
  const italian = text[DEFAULT_LANGUAGE];
  if (italian === undefined) throw new Error("a localized text without Italian");
  if (language === DEFAULT_LANGUAGE) {
    return { template: italian, language, source: "reference", isCurated: true };
  }
  const curated = text[language];
  if (curated !== undefined) {
    return { template: curated, language, source: "curated", isCurated: true };
  }
  const hash = sourceHash(italian);
  const cached = await readCache(services.cache, hash, language);
  if (cached) {
    return { template: cached.text, language, source: "cache", isCurated: cached.reviewed };
  }
  const fallback: ResolvedTemplate = {
    template: italian,
    language: DEFAULT_LANGUAGE,
    source: "fallback",
    isCurated: true,
  };
  let translation: Translation;
  try {
    translation = await services.translator.translate({
      text: italian,
      sourceLanguage: DEFAULT_LANGUAGE,
      targetLanguage: language,
      kind,
    });
  } catch {
    return fallback;
  }
  if (translation.text.trim() === "" || !samePlaceholders(italian, translation.text)) {
    return fallback;
  }
  await writeCache(services.cache, hash, language, {
    text: translation.text,
    reviewed: false,
    provider: translation.provider,
    model: translation.model,
  });
  return { template: translation.text, language, source: "translated", isCurated: false };
}

// A cache failure never blocks a reply (D72): the store logs its own errors (plan 08).
async function readCache(
  cache: TranslationCache,
  hash: string,
  language: LanguageCode,
): Promise<CachedTranslation | undefined> {
  try {
    return await cache.get(hash, language);
  } catch {
    return undefined;
  }
}

async function writeCache(
  cache: TranslationCache,
  hash: string,
  language: LanguageCode,
  entry: CachedTranslation,
): Promise<void> {
  try {
    await cache.set(hash, language, entry);
  } catch {
    // Same as readCache: the translation is still sent.
  }
}
```

```ts
import type { ConsentStep } from "../campaigns/schema/steps";

/** The consent step whose text `ref` is (D74), inline or through the campaign's texts. */
export function consentStepOf(
  ref: TextRef,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
): ConsentStep | undefined {
  return campaign.flow.find(
    (step): step is ConsentStep =>
      step.type === "consent" &&
      ((ref.kind === "step" && ref.path === step.id) ||
        (ref.kind === "campaign" && "ref" in step.text && step.text.ref === ref.key)),
  );
}

/** Where the official text is: the step's link, else the data controller's privacy page. */
export function officialLink(step: ConsentStep, campaign: CampaignDefinition): string {
  return step.link ?? campaign.dataController.privacyUrl;
```

```ts
import type { CampaignDefinition } from "../campaigns/schema/campaign";
import type { Effect } from "../engine/effects";
import type { TextParamValue, TextParams, TextRef } from "../engine/text-ref";
import type { FieldDefinition } from "../profile/field-definition";
import { resolveField } from "../profile/field-ref";
import { consentStepOf, officialLink } from "./critical";
import { fieldLabel } from "./field-labels";
import type { LanguageCode, LocalizedText } from "./localized-text";
import {
  SYSTEM_CATALOG,
  catalogText,
  type CatalogEntry,
  type CatalogKey,
  type I18nTextKey,
} from "./system-catalog";
import { interpolate } from "./template";
import {
  resolveTemplate,
  type ResolvedTemplate,
  type TemplateServices,
  type TemplateSource,
} from "./translate-template";

export interface TextTarget {
  /** The definition of the version the session is pinned to. */
  readonly campaign: CampaignDefinition;
  readonly language: LanguageCode;
}

export type TextSource = TemplateSource | "generated";

export interface ResolvedText {
  readonly text: string;
  /** The language actually sent: Italian after a fallback (D72). */
  readonly language: LanguageCode;
  readonly source: TextSource;
  /** Reviewed by a person (D73): runtime stores it in Consent.textShownIsCurated. */
  readonly isCurated: boolean;
  /** A consent text (D74). */
  readonly critical: boolean;
  /** Placeholders with no parameter, left visible (D71): runtime logs them. */
  readonly missingParams: readonly string[];
}

export type SendTextEffect = Extract<Effect, { type: "SendText" }>;

export interface TextResolver {
  resolve(ref: TextRef, target: TextTarget): Promise<ResolvedText>;
  /** A SendText effect, with the line its purpose adds (D76). */
  resolveSend(effect: SendTextEffect, target: TextTarget): Promise<ResolvedText>;
}

/** D77: the session language, or the campaign default while it is not chosen yet. */
export function outputLanguage(
  sessionLanguage: LanguageCode | null,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
): LanguageCode {
  return sessionLanguage ?? campaign.defaultLanguage;
}

function campaignText(campaign: CampaignDefinition, key: string): LocalizedText {
  const text = campaign.texts[key];
  if (!text) throw new Error(`unknown campaign text "${key}"`);
  return text;
}

function formatDate(value: string): string {
  const match = /^(\d{4})-(\d{2})-(\d{2})$/.exec(value);
  if (!match) return value;
  const [, year = "", month = "", day = ""] = match;
  return `${day}/${month}/${year}`;
}

function rendered(
  template: ResolvedTemplate,
  values: Readonly<Record<string, string>>,
  critical: boolean,
): ResolvedText {
  const { text, missing } = interpolate(template.template, values);
  return {
    text,
    language: template.language,
    source: template.source,
    isCurated: template.isCurated,
    critical,
    missingParams: missing,
  };
}

export function createTextResolver(services: TemplateServices): TextResolver {
  async function word(key: I18nTextKey, language: LanguageCode): Promise<string> {
    const entry = SYSTEM_CATALOG[key];
    return (await resolveTemplate(catalogText(entry, {}), language, "system", services)).template;
  }

  /** D75: how a value is shown; dates need the field's definition. */
  async function formatValue(
    value: TextParamValue,
    field: FieldDefinition | undefined,
    language: LanguageCode,
  ): Promise<string> {
    if (value === null) return word("format.empty", language);
    if (typeof value === "boolean") return word(value ? "format.yes" : "format.no", language);
    if (typeof value === "number") return String(value);
    if (typeof value === "string") return field?.type === "date" ? formatDate(value) : value;
    return value.join(", ");
  }

  async function formatPlain(
    params: TextParams,
    language: LanguageCode,
  ): Promise<Record<string, string>> {
    const values: Record<string, string> = {};
    for (const [name, value] of Object.entries(params)) {
      values[name] = await formatValue(value, undefined, language);
    }
    return values;
  }

  async function formatCatalogParams(
    entry: CatalogEntry,
    params: TextParams,
    language: LanguageCode,
    campaign: CampaignDefinition,
  ): Promise<Record<string, string>> {
    const fieldParam = Object.entries(entry.params).find(([, kind]) => kind === "field")?.[0];
    const fieldRef = fieldParam === undefined ? undefined : params[fieldParam];
    const field =
      typeof fieldRef === "string" ? resolveField(fieldRef, campaign.customFields) : undefined;
    const values: Record<string, string> = {};
    for (const [name, kind] of Object.entries(entry.params)) {
      const value = params[name];
      if (kind === "flag" || value === undefined) continue;
      if (kind === "field" && typeof value === "string") {
        const label = fieldLabel(value, campaign.customFields);
        values[name] = (await resolveTemplate(label, language, "label", services)).template;
      } else {
        values[name] = await formatValue(value, kind === "value" ? field : undefined, language);
      }
    }
    return values;
  }

  async function resolveSystem(
    key: CatalogKey,
    params: TextParams,
    target: TextTarget,
  ): Promise<ResolvedText> {
    const entry = SYSTEM_CATALOG[key];
    const template = await resolveTemplate(
      catalogText(entry, params),
      target.language,
      "system",
      services,
    );
    const values = await formatCatalogParams(entry, params, template.language, target.campaign);
    return rendered(template, values, false);
  }

  async function resolve(ref: TextRef, target: TextTarget): Promise<ResolvedText> {
    if (ref.kind === "generated") {
      return {
        text: ref.text,
        language: target.language,
        source: "generated",
        isCurated: false,
        critical: false,
        missingParams: [],
      };
    }
    if (ref.kind === "system") return resolveSystem(ref.key, ref.params ?? {}, target);
    const text = ref.kind === "campaign" ? campaignText(target.campaign, ref.key) : ref.text;
    const consent = consentStepOf(ref, target.campaign);
    const template = await resolveTemplate(
      text,
      target.language,
      consent ? "critical" : "campaign",
      services,
    );
    const values = await formatPlain(ref.params ?? {}, template.language);
    const result = rendered(template, values, consent !== undefined);
    if (!consent || result.isCurated) return result;
    // D74: never a machine translation of a consent alone.
    const link = await resolveSystem(
      "consent.official_link",
      { link: officialLink(consent, target.campaign) },
      target,
    );
    return { ...result, text: `${result.text}\n\n${link.text}` };
  }

  function leadFor(
    effect: SendTextEffect,
    target: TextTarget,
  ): Promise<ResolvedText> | undefined {
    if (effect.purpose === "rephrase") return resolveSystem("send.rephrase", {}, target);
    const missing = effect.text.kind === "generated" ? undefined : effect.text.params?.["missing"];
    if (effect.purpose === "clarify" && typeof missing === "string") {
      return resolveSystem("send.clarify", { missing }, target);
    }
    return undefined;
  }

  async function resolveSend(effect: SendTextEffect, target: TextTarget): Promise<ResolvedText> {
    const main = await resolve(effect.text, target);
    const pending = leadFor(effect, target);
    if (!pending) return main;
    const lead = await pending;
    return {
      ...main,
      text: `${lead.text}\n${main.text}`,
      isCurated: main.isCurated && lead.isCurated,
      missingParams: [...lead.missingParams, ...main.missingParams],
    };
  }

  return { resolve, resolveSend };
}
```

```ts
import type { CampaignDefinition } from "../../campaigns/schema/campaign";
import { isNoKeyword, isYesKeyword } from "../../engine/global-intents";
import type { Interpretation } from "../../engine/interpretation";
import { normalizeText } from "../../engine/normalize-text";
import type { SessionState } from "../../engine/state";
import type { FieldDefinition } from "../../profile/field-definition";
import { resolveField } from "../../profile/field-ref";

/** Nothing understood yet. Confidence 0.5: below D41's 0.8, the engine keeps its default. */
export function baseInterpretation(
  state: SessionState,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
): Interpretation {
  return {
    language: { code: state.language ?? campaign.defaultLanguage, confidence: 0.5 },
    intent: "answer",
    patch: [],
    currentQuestion: { completeness: "absent" },
  };
}

export interface CurrentField {
  readonly ref: string;
  readonly definition: FieldDefinition;
}

/** The field of the question waiting for an answer, if any. */
export function currentField(
  state: SessionState,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
): CurrentField | undefined {
  if (state.phase !== "flow" || state.questionId === null) return undefined;
  const step = campaign.flow.find((s) => s.id === state.stepId);
  if (step?.type !== "questions") return undefined;
  const question = step.questions.find((q) => q.id === state.questionId);
  if (!question) return undefined;
  const definition = resolveField(question.field, campaign.customFields);
  return definition ? { ref: question.field, definition } : undefined;
}

function toIsoDate(text: string): string | undefined {
  const italian = /^(\d{1,2})[/.-](\d{1,2})[/.-](\d{4})$/.exec(text);
  if (italian) {
    const [, day = "", month = "", year = ""] = italian;
    return `${year}-${month.padStart(2, "0")}-${day.padStart(2, "0")}`;
  }
  return /^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$/.test(text) ? text : undefined;
}

/** Text typed in the terminal → a value of the field's type; undefined when it is not one. */
export function coerceValue(text: string, field: FieldDefinition): unknown {
  const trimmed = text.trim();
  switch (field.type) {
    case "bool":
      if (isYesKeyword(trimmed)) return true;
      return isNoKeyword(trimmed) ? false : undefined;
    case "number": {
      const value = Number(trimmed.replace(",", "."));
      return trimmed !== "" && Number.isFinite(value) ? value : undefined;
    }
    case "date":
      return toIsoDate(trimmed);
    case "enum":
      return field.options?.find((option) => normalizeText(option) === normalizeText(trimmed));
    case "list": {
      const items = trimmed
        .split(/[,;]/)
        .map((item) => item.trim())
        .filter((item) => item !== "");
      return items.length > 0 ? items : undefined;
    }
    case "text":
      if (trimmed === "") return undefined;
      if (field.format === "iso-country") return trimmed.toUpperCase();
      if (field.format === "iso-language") return trimmed.toLowerCase();
      return trimmed;
  }
}
```

```ts
import type { ChoiceStep } from "../../campaigns/schema/steps";
import { isNoKeyword, isYesKeyword } from "../../engine/global-intents";
import type { Interpretation } from "../../engine/interpretation";
import { normalizeText } from "../../engine/normalize-text";
import type { InterpretRequest, Interpreter } from "../../nlu/interpreter";
import { baseInterpretation, coerceValue, currentField } from "./interpretations";

function yesNo(text: string): "yes" | "no" | undefined {
  if (isYesKeyword(text)) return "yes";
  return isNoKeyword(text) ? "no" : undefined;
}

/** An option id, its 1-based position, or yes/no for options called "yes"/"no". */
function matchOption(step: ChoiceStep, text: string): string | undefined {
  const normalized = normalizeText(text);
  const byId = step.options.find((o) => normalizeText(o.id) === normalized);
  if (byId) return byId.id;
  const position = Number(normalized);
  if (Number.isInteger(position) && position >= 1) return step.options[position - 1]?.id;
  const answer = yesNo(text);
  return answer && step.options.some((o) => o.id === answer) ? answer : undefined;
}

/** A deterministic stand-in for the LLM (D79): plain answers only, the rest via commands. */
export function naiveInterpret({ text, state, campaign }: InterpretRequest): Interpretation {
  const base = baseInterpretation(state, campaign);
  const unclear: Interpretation = { ...base, intent: "unclear" };
  const choice = (id: string): Interpretation => ({ ...base, intent: "choice", choice: id });
  const answer = yesNo(text);
  if (state.pendingConfirmation && answer) return choice(answer);
  // Post-completion with a cv_offer re-proposed (D44): a plain yes/no.
  if (state.phase === "post_completion") return answer ? choice(answer) : unclear;
  const step = campaign.flow.find((s) => s.id === state.stepId);
  if (state.phase !== "flow" || !step) return unclear;
  switch (step.type) {
    case "consent":
      return answer ? choice(answer === "yes" ? "accepted" : "declined") : unclear;
    case "cv_offer":
      return answer ? choice(answer) : unclear;
    case "choice": {
      const option = matchOption(step, text);
      return option ? choice(option) : unclear;
    }
    case "questions": {
      const current = currentField(state, campaign);
      const value = current ? coerceValue(text, current.definition) : undefined;
      if (!current || value === undefined) return unclear;
      return {
        ...base,
        patch: [{ field: current.ref, value, confidence: 0.95, evidence: text }],
        currentQuestion: { completeness: "complete" },
      };
    }
    case "message":
    case "end":
      return unclear;
  }
}

export const naiveInterpreter: Interpreter = {
  interpret: (request) => Promise.resolve(naiveInterpret(request)),
};
```

```ts
import type { CampaignDefinition } from "../../campaigns/schema/campaign";
import { InterpretationSchema, type Interpretation } from "../../engine/interpretation";
import type { SessionState } from "../../engine/state";
import { resolveField } from "../../profile/field-ref";
import { baseInterpretation, coerceValue, currentField } from "./interpretations";

export type SimulatorInput =
  /** Sent to the interpreter. */
  | { readonly kind: "text"; readonly text: string }
  /** Sent straight to the engine, whatever the interpreter (D79). */
  | {
      readonly kind: "forced";
      readonly text: string;
      readonly interpretation: Interpretation | null;
    }
  | { readonly kind: "state" }
  | { readonly kind: "usage" }
  | { readonly kind: "quit" }
  | { readonly kind: "invalid"; readonly message: string };

export const USAGE: readonly string[] = [
  "Type the candidate's message, or a command:",
  "  /choice <id>           pick an option (yes, no, accepted, declined, an option id, regenerate_cv)",
  "  /low <value>           answer the current question with low confidence (asks a confirmation)",
  "  /partial <missing>     partial answer: the bot asks for <missing>",
  "  /edit <field> <value>  ask to change a field (e.g. /edit location Bologna)",
  "  /offtopic [reply]      off-topic message, with an optional generated reply",
  "  /help                  the candidate asks for help",
  "  /refuse                the candidate refuses to answer",
  "  /unclear               the NLU could not tell",
  "  /null <text>           NLU unavailable: only the keywords in <text> count",
  "  /nlu <json>            a full Interpretation as JSON",
  "  /state                 show the session state and the profile",
  "  /?                     this help",
  "  /quit                  stop",
];

const invalid = (message: string): SimulatorInput => ({ kind: "invalid", message });
const forced = (text: string, interpretation: Interpretation | null): SimulatorInput => ({
  kind: "forced",
  text,
  interpretation,
});

export function parseInput(
  line: string,
  state: SessionState,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
): SimulatorInput {
  const trimmed = line.trim();
  if (trimmed === "") return invalid("empty message: type an answer, or /? for the commands");
  if (!trimmed.startsWith("/")) return { kind: "text", text: trimmed };
  const command = trimmed.split(/\s+/, 1)[0] ?? "";
  const arg = trimmed.slice(command.length).trim();
  const base = baseInterpretation(state, campaign);
  switch (command) {
    case "/quit":
    case "/q":
      return { kind: "quit" };
    case "/state":
      return { kind: "state" };
    case "/?":
      return { kind: "usage" };
    case "/null":
      return forced(arg, null);
    case "/choice":
      return arg ? forced(arg, { ...base, intent: "choice", choice: arg }) : invalid("/choice needs an option id");
    case "/help":
      return forced(arg, { ...base, intent: "help" });
    case "/refuse":
      return forced(arg, { ...base, intent: "refusal" });
    case "/unclear":
      return forced(arg, { ...base, intent: "unclear" });
    case "/offtopic":
      return forced(
        arg,
        arg ? { ...base, intent: "off_topic", offTopicReply: arg } : { ...base, intent: "off_topic" },
      );
    case "/partial":
      return arg
        ? forced(arg, { ...base, currentQuestion: { completeness: "partial", missing: arg } })
        : invalid("/partial needs what is missing");
    case "/low":
      return lowConfidence(arg, state, campaign, base);
    case "/edit":
      return editRequest(arg, campaign, base);
    case "/nlu":
      return rawInterpretation(arg);
    default:
      return invalid(`unknown command ${command}: /? lists the commands`);
  }
}

function lowConfidence(
  arg: string,
  state: SessionState,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
  base: Interpretation,
): SimulatorInput {
  const current = currentField(state, campaign);
  if (!current) return invalid("/low works only while a question is waiting");
  const value = coerceValue(arg, current.definition);
  if (value === undefined) return invalid(`"${arg}" is not a valid ${current.definition.type}`);
  return forced(arg, {
    ...base,
    patch: [{ field: current.ref, value, confidence: 0.5, evidence: arg }],
    currentQuestion: { completeness: "complete" },
  });
}

/** An unknown field is sent anyway: the engine rejects it (edit_invalid), as with the LLM. */
function editRequest(
  arg: string,
  campaign: CampaignDefinition,
  base: Interpretation,
): SimulatorInput {
  const field = arg.split(/\s+/, 1)[0] ?? "";
  const raw = arg.slice(field.length).trim();
  if (field === "" || raw === "") return invalid("/edit needs a field and a value");
  const definition = resolveField(field, campaign.customFields);
  const newValue = (definition && coerceValue(raw, definition)) ?? raw;
  return forced(arg, { ...base, intent: "edit_request", editTarget: { field, newValue } });
}

function rawInterpretation(arg: string): SimulatorInput {
  let json: unknown;
  try {
    json = JSON.parse(arg);
  } catch {
    return invalid("/nlu needs a JSON object");
  }
  const parsed = InterpretationSchema.safeParse(json);
  if (!parsed.success) {
    const issues = parsed.error.issues.map((i) => `${i.path.join(".") || "(root)"}: ${i.message}`);
    return invalid(`not an Interpretation: ${issues.join("; ")}`);
  }
  return forced("", parsed.data);
}
```

Nota su `editRequest`: `definition && coerceValue(...)` vale `undefined` sia per un campo
sconosciuto sia per un valore non convertibile; in entrambi i casi si invia il testo così
com'è e decide il motore (`validatePatch`). Attenzione a `false`: `coerceValue` può restituire
`false` per un booleano, e `false ?? raw` resta `false` (l'operatore `??` scatta solo su
`null`/`undefined`), quindi `/edit drivingLicense no` invia `false` come atteso.

```ts
import type { CampaignDefinition } from "../../campaigns/schema/campaign";
import { describeEffect } from "../../engine/describe";
import type { Effect } from "../../engine/effects";
import type { EngineEvent } from "../../engine/events";
import type { Interpretation } from "../../engine/interpretation";
import { initialSessionState, type SessionState } from "../../engine/state";
import { step } from "../../engine/step";
import {
  outputLanguage,
  type ResolvedText,
  type TextResolver,
  type TextSource,
} from "../../i18n/resolve";
import type { Interpreter } from "../../nlu/interpreter";
import { parseInput, USAGE } from "./commands";

export interface SimulationOptions {
  readonly campaign: CampaignDefinition;
  readonly interpreter: Interpreter;
  readonly resolver: TextResolver;
  /** ISO-8601: the engine never reads the clock. */
  readonly now: () => string;
  /** Earlier user messages passed to the interpreter (spec §8.2). */
  readonly historySize: number;
}

export interface Simulation {
  readonly state: SessionState;
  start(): Promise<string[]>;
  /** The lines to print; null when the operator asked to quit. */
  handle(line: string): Promise<string[] | null>;
}

const TAGGED_SOURCES: ReadonlySet<TextSource> = new Set([
  "cache",
  "translated",
  "fallback",
  "generated",
]);

/** "bot> text", with the source when the text was not written by a person. */
export function formatBotText(resolved: ResolvedText): string[] {
  const label = TAGGED_SOURCES.has(resolved.source) ? `bot[${resolved.source}]> ` : "bot> ";
  const [first = "", ...rest] = resolved.text.split("\n");
  return [`${label}${first}`, ...rest.map((line) => `${" ".repeat(label.length)}${line}`)];
}

export function describeState(state: SessionState): string[] {
  const lines = [
    [
      `phase=${state.phase}`,
      `step=${state.stepId ?? "-"}`,
      `question=${state.questionId ?? "-"}`,
      `language=${state.language ?? "-"}`,
      `consentsAccepted=${String(state.consentsAccepted)}`,
      `clarifications=${state.clarifications}`,
      `pending=${state.pendingConfirmation?.kind ?? "none"}`,
    ].join(" "),
  ];
  for (const [field, s] of Object.entries(state.profile)) {
    lines.push(`  ${field} = ${JSON.stringify(s.value)} (${s.status}, ${s.confidence})`);
  }
  return lines;
}

export function createSimulation(options: SimulationOptions): Simulation {
  const { campaign } = options;
  let state = initialSessionState();
  const history: string[] = [];

  async function render(effects: readonly Effect[]): Promise<string[]> {
    // D77: after step(), so a turn that sets the language already speaks it.
    const target = { campaign, language: outputLanguage(state.language, campaign) };
    const lines: string[] = [];
    for (const effect of effects) {
      if (effect.type === "SendText") {
        lines.push(...formatBotText(await options.resolver.resolveSend(effect, target)));
      } else {
        lines.push(`  · ${describeEffect(effect)}`);
      }
    }
    return lines;
  }

  async function run(event: EngineEvent): Promise<string[]> {
    const result = step(state, event, campaign);
    state = result.state;
    return render(result.effects);
  }

  async function interpret(text: string): Promise<[Interpretation | null, string[]]> {
    try {
      const interpretation = await options.interpreter.interpret({
        text,
        state,
        campaign,
        history: history.slice(-options.historySize),
        turnId: null,
      });
      return [interpretation, []];
    } catch (error: unknown) {
      // Like runtime: a failing nlu is an unavailable nlu (D32), the engine still answers.
      const message = error instanceof Error ? error.message : String(error);
      return [null, [`! interpreter failed: ${message}`]];
    }
  }

  return {
    get state() {
      return state;
    },
    start: () => run({ type: "session_started", at: options.now() }),
    async handle(line) {
      const input = parseInput(line, state, campaign);
      switch (input.kind) {
        case "quit":
          return null;
        case "usage":
          return [...USAGE];
        case "state":
          return describeState(state);
        case "invalid":
          return [`! ${input.message}`];
        case "forced":
          return run({
            type: "user_turn",
            at: options.now(),
            text: input.text,
            interpretation: input.interpretation,
          });
        case "text": {
          const [interpretation, notes] = await interpret(input.text);
          history.push(input.text);
          const lines = await run({
            type: "user_turn",
            at: options.now(),
            text: input.text,
            interpretation,
          });
          return [...notes, ...lines];
        }
      }
    },
  };
}
```

```ts
import { InvalidArgumentError, Option, type Command } from "commander";
import { validateCampaign } from "../../campaigns/validate";
import { createTextResolver } from "../../i18n/resolve";
import { MemoryTranslationCache } from "../../i18n/translation-cache";
import { CliError, type CliDeps } from "../deps";
import { formatIssues } from "../format-issues";
import { readCampaignFile } from "../read-campaign-file";
import {
  INTERPRETER_KINDS,
  TRANSLATOR_KINDS,
  createSimulationServices,
  type SimulationKinds,
} from "../simulate/services";
import { createSimulation } from "../simulate/simulation";

interface SimulateOptions extends SimulationKinds {
  readonly history: number;
}

function parseHistory(value: string): number {
  const size = Number(value);
  if (!Number.isInteger(size) || size < 0) {
    throw new InvalidArgumentError("must be a whole number, 0 or more");
  }
  return size;
}

export function registerSimulateCommand(campaigns: Command, deps: CliDeps): void {
  campaigns
    .command("simulate")
    .description("Run an interview in the terminal on a campaign file (no database, no WhatsApp)")
    .argument("<file>", "campaign JSON file")
    .addOption(
      new Option("--nlu <kind>", "how answers are interpreted")
        .choices(INTERPRETER_KINDS)
        .default("naive"),
    )
    .addOption(
      new Option("--translator <kind>", "how texts are translated")
        .choices(TRANSLATOR_KINDS)
        .default("fake"),
    )
    .option("--history <n>", "earlier user messages given to the NLU", parseHistory, 3)
    .action(async (file: string, options: SimulateOptions) => {
      const result = validateCampaign(await readCampaignFile(deps, file));
      if (!result.ok) {
        deps.err(`✗ ${file}: ${result.issues.length} issue(s)`);
        formatIssues(result.issues).forEach(deps.err);
        throw new CliError(`Invalid campaign: ${file}`);
      }
      const services = createSimulationServices(options, deps);
      const simulation = createSimulation({
        campaign: result.campaign,
        interpreter: services.interpreter,
        resolver: createTextResolver({
          translator: services.translator,
          cache: new MemoryTranslationCache(),
        }),
        now: deps.now,
        historySize: options.history,
      });
      deps.out(
        `Simulating "${result.campaign.campaign}" (nlu=${options.nlu}, translator=${options.translator}). Type /? for the commands, /quit to stop.`,
      );
      (await simulation.start()).forEach(deps.out);
      const input = deps.openInput();
      try {
        for (;;) {
          const line = await input.next("you> ");
          if (line === null) break;
          const reply = await simulation.handle(line);
          if (reply === null) break;
          reply.forEach(deps.out);
        }
      } finally {
        input.close();
      }
    });
}
```

- **Piano 06.** Implementa `Translator` (`createLlmTranslator`) e `Interpreter`
  (`createLlmInterpreter`) e aggiunge `"llm"` a `INTERPRETER_KINDS` / `TRANSLATOR_KINDS`. L'NLU

- **Piano 08.** Crea la tabella `TranslationCache` (implementazione di `TranslationCache` in
  `store`) e usa `createTextResolver` nel runtime. I testi di un turno vanno risolti nella
  lingua di `state` **dopo** `step()` (D77). `RecordConsent.text` va risolto con `resolve()`,
  salvando `text` e `isCurated` in `Consent` (D73). `missingParams` non vuoto va loggato con la
  chiave del testo (`describeTextRef`), mai con il testo.
- **Campagna standard (piano 03, D69).** Ora che i18n interpola `{dataController.name}` e
  `{dataController.privacyUrl}`, i testi dei consensi possono usarli al posto del testo per
  esteso. È un cambiamento di contenuto, da fare con una nuova versione della campagna.
- **`requireCurated` (§9).** Non è nello schema della campagna e non è implementato: va deciso
  cosa fare quando manca la traduzione curata richiesta (italiano + link? blocco?).

  stessa domanda (D76).
- **Formato dei numeri e delle liste per lingua.** I numeri escono con `String()` e le liste con
  la virgola in ogni lingua: per l'arabo e le lingue con separatori diversi va valutato con gli
  evals di traduzione (piano 07).
