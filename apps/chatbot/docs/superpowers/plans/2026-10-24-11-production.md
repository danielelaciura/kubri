# Piano 11 — Messa in produzione e migrazione da Make

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rendere il chatbot esercibile in produzione: dati che scadono e si cancellano davvero
(anche verso la dashboard e lo storage), avvisi quando qualcosa si rompe, strumenti per
diagnosticarlo, un'immagine di deploy, le campagne dei clienti portate sul nuovo sistema e una
guida per passare il numero Kubri da Make al chatbot.

**Architecture:** Tutto il lavoro nuovo è fatto da **job pianificati** (`sweep-inactive`,
`monitor`) e da un job a richiesta (`delete-contact`), sulla stessa coda pg-boss del piano 08. Le
decisioni (che cosa scade, che cosa va segnalato) sono **funzioni pure** provate con esempi;
il database e la rete restano ai bordi, dietro interfacce già esistenti (`KubriClient`,
`CvStorage`) più una nuova (`AlertSink`). Il comando `ops` legge soltanto, salvo due azioni
esplicite sugli scarti (`retry`, `discard`).

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript strict, Prisma 7, pg-boss (piano 08), Zod 4, Vitest 4, Docker.
Nessuna dipendenza nuova di terze parti (solo `tsx` passa da sviluppo a produzione, D140).

**Spec:** §10.6 (conservazione e cancellazione), §12.1, §13 e §13.1 (errori e osservabilità),
§16 (infrastruttura e sicurezza), §18 (punti 4, 6, 8, 12), D17, D24, D57, D109.

**Prerequisito:** piani 01–06, 08, 09 e 10 implementati. Il 07 (evals) non serve. Si lavora
**direttamente sul branch ombrello `docs/chatbot-spec-into-monorepo`**: nessun branch nuovo,

## Decisioni prese in questo piano (da riportare nella spec, §2, come D132–D142)

Il piano 10 ha preso D122–D131.

- **D132 — Durate configurabili, valori iniziali provvisori.** Variabili d'ambiente, tutte con un
  valore di partenza che il titolare del trattamento deve confermare (§18.6): sessione iniziata
  e mai oltre i consensi 48 ore; sessione oltre i consensi senza risposte 7 giorni → `abandoned`;
  sessione `abandoned` 90 giorni; tracce NLU 30 giorni; corpi dei messaggi 90 giorni; payload
  grezzi dei messaggi 14 giorni; scarti dell'ingresso 30 giorni.
- **D133 — `sweep-inactive`.** Pianificato ogni ora. La decisione è una funzione pura
  (`planSweep`): prima dei consensi → cancella il contatto (D24); dopo i consensi → `abandoned`;
  `abandoned` scaduta → cancella il contatto. Una `needs_attention` non si tocca mai: la decide un
  operatore. Un contatto con un'altra sessione recente non si cancella.
- **D134 — Scadenza dei dati.** Lo stesso job svuota ciò che ha una durata propria: tracce NLU
  (cancellate), corpi e payload grezzi dei messaggi (azzerati, la riga resta), scarti
  dell'ingresso (cancellati). I messaggi in uscita ancora da inviare non si toccano.
- **D135 — `delete-contact`.** Ordine fisso, ripetibile: (1) propaga a Kubri, (2) cancella gli
  oggetti dello storage, (3) cancella il contatto (a cascata) in una transazione. Un guasto
  temporaneo ritenta il job intero; uno definitivo lo manda nella coda degli scarti **senza**
  aver cancellato nulla in locale, così la prova della richiesta resta. `Consent` sopravvive (D109).
- **D136 — Propagazione a Kubri (chiude §18.8).** Stessa strada del webhook, metodo `DELETE`:
  `DELETE /api/webhooks/make/candidate` con `{ key, externalKey }` e lo stesso segreto. La
  dashboard cancella il candidato del pool (le note vanno a cascata, l'embedding sta nella
  riga) e scrive un `AuditLog` senza dati personali. Idempotente: cancellare ciò che non c'è è un
  successo. Non serve un evento nuovo né un endpoint con un altro percorso.
- **D137 — Avvisi.** Interfaccia `AlertSink`; in produzione un indirizzo (`ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL`,
  compatibile con Slack, Mattermost e Discord) che riceve un riepilogo **di soli conteggi**. Il job
  `monitor` (ogni 5 minuti) conta cosa è successo nell'ultima finestra: scarti nelle code,
  sessioni in `needs_attention`, messaggi `failed`, scarti dell'ingresso. Senza stato: ogni
  fatto è segnalato una volta, a costo di perderne qualcuno se il job salta un giro. Il
  servizio UE di monitoraggio degli errori (§13.1) si sceglie con l'hosting (Punti aperti).
- **D138 — Comando `ops`.** `ops session <id>` (cronologia, tracce, effetti, senza testi),
  `ops dead-letters list|retry|discard`, `ops stats`. Legge soltanto, tranne `retry` e
  `discard`, che chiedono la conferma `--yes`.
- **D139 — Vitalità del worker.** Il worker espone `GET /health` (200 se il database risponde e
  pg-boss è avviato) su `WORKER_HEALTH_PORT`; la piattaforma lo usa per riavviarlo. Con
  `REQUIRE_INTEGRATIONS=true` (produzione) il worker **rifiuta di partire** se mancano Kubri,
  storage o avvisi.
- **D140 — Immagine unica.** Un `Dockerfile` che fa partire `web` o `worker` secondo il comando,
  senza legarsi a un fornitore. Il codice gira con `tsx` (le importazioni senza estensione non
  si compilano per Node ESM): `tsx` diventa dipendenza di produzione. La CI costruisce l'immagine
  ma non la pubblica.
- **D141 — Campagne dei clienti.** Un test controlla che **ogni** file in `campaigns/` si
  validi e si importi; ogni campagna portata ha il suo file e i suoi scenari. I contenuti
  arrivano dal proprietario del cliente: il piano dà la procedura e lo stampo, non i testi.
- **D142 — Passaggio per numero.** Prima un numero di test, poi il numero Kubri (D17). Le
  sessioni già aperte su Make finiscono su Make. Il ritorno indietro è riportare l'indirizzo del
  webhook di Meta su Make: la guida lo scrive come passo, non come eccezione.

```ts
import { z } from "zod";

const days = (fallback: number) => z.coerce.number().int().min(1).max(3650).default(fallback);

const EnvSchema = z.object({
  PRE_CONSENT_TTL_HOURS: z.coerce.number().int().min(1).max(24 * 365).default(48),
  INACTIVITY_ABANDON_DAYS: days(7),
  ABANDONED_RETENTION_DAYS: days(90),
  NLU_TRACE_RETENTION_DAYS: days(30),
  MESSAGE_BODY_RETENTION_DAYS: days(90),
  PAYLOAD_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS: days(14),
  DEAD_LETTER_RETENTION_DAYS: days(30),
});

export interface RetentionConfig {
  readonly preConsentTtlHours: number;
  readonly inactivityAbandonDays: number;
  readonly abandonedRetentionDays: number;
  readonly nluTraceRetentionDays: number;
  readonly messageBodyRetentionDays: number;
  readonly payloadRawRetentionDays: number;
  readonly deadLetterRetentionDays: number;
}

/**
 * Spec §10.6, D132. The defaults are a starting point: the durations are the data controller's
 * decision (spec §18, point 6). An invalid value names the variable, never the value.
 */
export function parseRetentionConfig(source: Readonly<Record<string, string | undefined>>): RetentionConfig {
  const present = Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(source).filter(([, v]) => v !== undefined && v.trim() !== ""));
  const result = EnvSchema.safeParse(present);
  if (!result.success) {
    const names = result.error.issues.map((issue) => issue.path.join(".")).join(", ");
    throw new Error(`Invalid retention configuration: ${names}`);
  }
  const env = result.data;
  return {
    preConsentTtlHours: env.PRE_CONSENT_TTL_HOURS,
    inactivityAbandonDays: env.INACTIVITY_ABANDON_DAYS,
    abandonedRetentionDays: env.ABANDONED_RETENTION_DAYS,
    nluTraceRetentionDays: env.NLU_TRACE_RETENTION_DAYS,
    messageBodyRetentionDays: env.MESSAGE_BODY_RETENTION_DAYS,
    payloadRawRetentionDays: env.PAYLOAD_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS,
    deadLetterRetentionDays: env.DEAD_LETTER_RETENTION_DAYS,
  };
}

export interface SweepRow {
  readonly sessionId: string;
  readonly contactId: string;
  readonly status: "active" | "abandoned" | "needs_attention";
  /** The engine's `consentsAccepted` (spec §10.6, D24). */
  readonly consentsAccepted: boolean;
  readonly lastActivityAt: Date;
  /** The newest activity across every session of the same contact. */
  readonly contactLastActivityAt: Date;
}

export type SweepAction =
  | { readonly type: "delete_contact"; readonly contactId: string; readonly reason: "pre_consent_expired" | "abandoned_expired" }
  | { readonly type: "abandon"; readonly sessionId: string };

const HOUR_MS = 3600_000;
const DAY_MS = 24 * HOUR_MS;

/** D133: what to do with each session. Pure: dates and rows in, actions out. */
export function planSweep(rows: readonly SweepRow[], now: Date, config: RetentionConfig): SweepAction[] {
  const older = (date: Date, ms: number): boolean => now.getTime() - date.getTime() > ms;
  const actions: SweepAction[] = [];
  const deleting = new Set<string>();
  const remove = (row: SweepRow, reason: "pre_consent_expired" | "abandoned_expired", ttlMs: number) => {
    // Another, newer session of the same contact keeps the contact alive.
    if (!older(row.lastActivityAt, ttlMs) || !older(row.contactLastActivityAt, ttlMs)) return;
    if (deleting.has(row.contactId)) return;
    deleting.add(row.contactId);
    actions.push({ type: "delete_contact", contactId: row.contactId, reason });
  };

  for (const row of rows) {
    if (row.status === "needs_attention") continue;
    if (row.status === "abandoned") {
      remove(row, "abandoned_expired", config.abandonedRetentionDays * DAY_MS);
    } else if (!row.consentsAccepted) {
      remove(row, "pre_consent_expired", config.preConsentTtlHours * HOUR_MS);
    } else if (older(row.lastActivityAt, config.inactivityAbandonDays * DAY_MS)) {
      actions.push({ type: "abandon", sessionId: row.sessionId });
    }
  }
  return actions;
}
```

```ts
import type { RetentionConfig, SweepRow } from "../runtime/retention";
import type { Database, Tx } from "./db";

const HOUR_MS = 3600_000;
const DAY_MS = 24 * HOUR_MS;

const consentsAcceptedOf = (engineState: unknown): boolean =>
  typeof engineState === "object" &&
  engineState !== null &&
  "consentsAccepted" in engineState &&
  engineState.consentsAccepted === true;

/**
 * Sessions that could be due: active or abandoned, idle for longer than the shortest period.
 * `needs_attention` is left out on purpose (D133). The planner decides the rest.
 */
export async function loadSweepRows(db: Database, now: Date, config: RetentionConfig): Promise<SweepRow[]> {
  const shortest = Math.min(config.preConsentTtlHours * HOUR_MS, config.inactivityAbandonDays * DAY_MS);
  const sessions = await db.session.findMany({
    where: { status: { in: ["active", "abandoned"] }, lastActivityAt: { lt: new Date(now.getTime() - shortest) } },
    select: { id: true, contactId: true, status: true, lastActivityAt: true, engineState: true },
  });
  if (sessions.length === 0) return [];
  const newest = await db.session.groupBy({
    by: ["contactId"],
    where: { contactId: { in: [...new Set(sessions.map((x) => x.contactId))] } },
    _max: { lastActivityAt: true },
  });
  const latest = new Map(newest.map((n) => [n.contactId, n._max.lastActivityAt]));
  return sessions.map((x) => ({
    sessionId: x.id,
    contactId: x.contactId,
    status: x.status === "abandoned" ? "abandoned" : "active",
    consentsAccepted: consentsAcceptedOf(x.engineState),
    lastActivityAt: x.lastActivityAt,
    contactLastActivityAt: latest.get(x.contactId) ?? x.lastActivityAt,
  }));
}

/** true if this call changed the session; an already abandoned or closed one is left alone. */
export async function abandonSession(db: Database, sessionId: string): Promise<boolean> {
  const result = await db.session.updateMany({
    where: { id: sessionId, status: "active" },
    data: { status: "abandoned" },
  });
  return result.count === 1;
}

export interface ExpiryCounts {
  readonly traces: number;
  readonly bodies: number;
  readonly payloads: number;
  readonly deadLetters: number;
}

const WAITING_TO_SEND = { direction: "outbound", status: { in: ["queued", "sending"] } } as const;

/**
 * D134: what has a retention of its own. Bodies and raw payloads are blanked (the row stays for
 * order, delivery states and statistics); traces and dead letters are deleted. Outbound messages
 * still waiting to be sent are never touched.
 */
export async function expireData(db: Tx, config: RetentionConfig, now: Date): Promise<ExpiryCounts> {
  const before = (days: number) => new Date(now.getTime() - days * DAY_MS);
  const traces = await db.nluTrace.deleteMany({ where: { createdAt: { lt: before(config.nluTraceRetentionDays) } } });
  const bodies = await db.message.updateMany({
    where: {
      createdAt: { lt: before(config.messageBodyRetentionDays) },
      NOT: WAITING_TO_SEND,
      OR: [{ body: { not: null } }, { bodyRedacted: { not: null } }],
    },
    data: { body: null, bodyRedacted: null },
  });
  const payloads = await db.message.updateMany({
    where: {
      createdAt: { lt: before(config.payloadRawRetentionDays) },
      NOT: WAITING_TO_SEND,
      payloadRaw: { not: Prisma.DbNull },
    },
    data: { payloadRaw: Prisma.DbNull },
  });
  const deadLetters = await db.inboundDeadLetter.deleteMany({
    where: { createdAt: { lt: before(config.deadLetterRetentionDays) } },
  });
  return { traces: traces.count, bodies: bodies.count, payloads: payloads.count, deadLetters: deadLetters.count };
}
```

Note per chi implementa: se `NOT: WAITING_TO_SEND` non è accettato da TypeScript con un oggetto
`as const`, scriverlo come filtro inline `NOT: { direction: "outbound", status: { in: [...] } }`
(stesso significato, come nel `purgeSessionData` del piano 08). Se il filtro `payloadRaw: { not:
Prisma.DbNull }` non è accettato, usare `NOT: { payloadRaw: { equals: Prisma.DbNull } }`.

```ts
import type { Logger } from "pino";
import type { JobQueue } from "../../store/queue";
import { abandonSession, expireData, loadSweepRows, type ExpiryCounts } from "../../store/retention";
import type { Database } from "../../store/db";
import { planSweep, type RetentionConfig } from "../retention";

export interface SweepDeps {
  readonly db: Database;
  readonly logger: Logger;
  readonly queue: JobQueue;
  readonly config: RetentionConfig;
  readonly now: () => Date;
}

export interface SweepResult {
  readonly deleted: number;
  readonly abandoned: number;
  readonly expired: ExpiryCounts;
}

/**
 * D133, D134: abandons the sessions that went quiet, queues the deletion of the contacts whose
 * time is up, and blanks the data whose retention is over. Counts only in the log: no ids of
 * candidates, no content. Safe to run twice: every step ignores what is already done.
 */
export async function handleSweep(deps: SweepDeps): Promise<SweepResult> {
  const now = deps.now();
  const actions = planSweep(await loadSweepRows(deps.db, now, deps.config), now, deps.config);
  let deleted = 0;
  let abandoned = 0;
  for (const action of actions) {
    if (action.type === "delete_contact") {
      await deps.queue.enqueue("delete-contact", { contactId: action.contactId });
      deleted += 1;
    } else if (await abandonSession(deps.db, action.sessionId)) {
      abandoned += 1;
    }
  }
  const expired = await expireData(deps.db, deps.config, now);
  deps.logger.info({ deleted, abandoned, ...expired }, "sweep done");
  return { deleted, abandoned, expired };
}
```

Nota: se il sweep gira due volte prima che `delete-contact` abbia finito, accoda due
cancellazioni dello stesso contatto: la seconda trova il contatto già sparito e termina
(Task 6). Non serve un blocco.

```ts
/**
 * The chatbot's erasure requests (spec §10.6, D136). Same secret as the upsert. Idempotent:
 * deleting a candidate that is not there is a success, so a retry after a lost answer is safe.
 * The audit log records that someone was erased, never who (no personal data, no user).
 */
export async function DELETE(req: Request): Promise<Response> {
  const expected = process.env["MAKE_WEBHOOK_SECRET"];
  if (!expected || req.headers.get("authorization") !== `Bearer ${expected}`) {
    return NextResponse.json({ error: "unauthorized" }, { status: 401 });
  }

  let body: unknown;
  try {
    body = await req.json();
  } catch {
    return NextResponse.json({ error: "invalid_json" }, { status: 400 });
  }
  const parsed = candidateDeletionSchema.safeParse(body);
  if (!parsed.success) {
    return NextResponse.json({ error: "validation_failed", details: parsed.error.flatten() }, { status: 400 });
  }

  let pool;
  try {
    pool = await resolvePoolByExternalKey(parsed.data.externalKey);
  } catch (e) {
    if (e instanceof UnknownPoolError) {
      return NextResponse.json({ error: "unknown_pool", externalKey: e.externalKey }, { status: 422 });
    }
    throw e;
  }

  // Notes and tags go with the row (cascade); the embedding is a column of the row.
  const { count } = await prisma.candidate.deleteMany({
    where: { poolId: pool.id, externalId: parsed.data.key },
  });
  if (count > 0) {
    await prisma.auditLog.create({
      data: {
        userId: null,
        organizationId: null,
        action: "candidate.deleted_by_chatbot",
        resourceType: "Candidate",
        resourceId: pool.id,
        metadata: { source: "chatbot", count },
      },
    });
  }
  return NextResponse.json({ ok: true, deleted: count });
}
```

nel middleware (`/api/webhooks`), quindi non serve toccarlo (vale per il metodo come per il

```ts
import type { Logger } from "pino";
import { z } from "zod";
import type { KubriClient } from "../../integrations/kubri/client";
import type { CvStorage } from "../../integrations/storage";
import type { Database } from "../../store/db";
import type { JobPayloads, JobQueue } from "../../store/queue";
import { TransientJobError } from "./errors";

export interface DeleteContactDeps {
  readonly db: Database;
  readonly logger: Logger;
  readonly queue: JobQueue;
  /** null on a worker without Kubri configured: only local runs (REQUIRE_INTEGRATIONS=false). */
  readonly kubri: KubriClient | null;
  readonly storage: CvStorage | null;
}

export type DeleteContactResult = "deleted" | "skipped" | "dead";

const DefinitionSchema = z.object({ externalKey: z.string().min(1) });

/**
 * Spec §10.6, D135. From the outside in, every step safe to repeat:
 *  1. the dashboard (the key is `<contact>:<campaign>`, so it can only be rebuilt while the
 *     contact still exists: that is why the local rows go last),
 *  2. the CV objects,
 *  3. the contact and, by cascade, sessions, messages, profiles, traces and CV rows.
 * `Consent` rows stay as the minimal proof (D109). Logs carry ids and counts only.
 */
export async function handleDeleteContact(
  deps: DeleteContactDeps,
  job: JobPayloads["delete-contact"],
): Promise<DeleteContactResult> {
  const contact = await deps.db.contact.findUnique({
    where: { id: job.contactId },
    select: {
      id: true,
      sessions: {
        select: {
          campaignVersion: { select: { campaignId: true, definition: true } },
          profile: { select: { cvDocuments: { select: { storageKey: true } } } },
        },
      },
    },
  });
  if (!contact) {
    deps.logger.info({ contactId: job.contactId }, "delete-contact skipped: already gone");
    return "skipped";
  }

  const targets = new Map<string, { key: string; externalKey: string }>();
  const objects = new Set<string>();
  for (const session of contact.sessions) {
    const { campaignId, definition } = session.campaignVersion;
    const key = `${contact.id}:${campaignId}`;
    targets.set(key, { key, externalKey: DefinitionSchema.parse(definition).externalKey });
    for (const cv of session.profile?.cvDocuments ?? []) objects.add(cv.storageKey);
  }

  if (deps.kubri) {
    for (const target of targets.values()) {
      const result = await deps.kubri.delete(target);
      if (result.ok || (result.kind === "permanent" && result.code === "unknown_pool")) continue;
      if (result.kind === "transient") throw new TransientJobError(result.code);
      deps.logger.error({ contactId: contact.id, code: result.code, status: result.status }, "delete-contact refused by Kubri");
      await deps.queue.deadLetter("delete-contact", job, result.code);
      return "dead";
    }
  }

  if (objects.size > 0) {
    if (!deps.storage) throw new Error("delete-contact needs the CV storage: objects are left to remove");
    for (const key of objects) await deps.storage.remove(key);
  }

  await deps.db.contact.delete({ where: { id: contact.id } });
  deps.logger.info({ contactId: contact.id, kubri: targets.size, objects: objects.size }, "contact deleted");
  return "deleted";
}
```

```ts
export interface Signals {
  /** Jobs that were sent to a `*-dead` queue in the window, by queue name. */
  readonly deadJobs: Readonly<Record<string, number>>;
  readonly needsAttention: number;
  readonly failedMessages: number;
  readonly inboundDeadLetters: number;
}

export interface Alert {
  readonly severity: "warning" | "critical";
  readonly title: string;
  readonly lines: readonly string[];
}

/**
 * D137: what is worth waking someone for. Words and numbers only: an alert is read in a chat
 * tool outside our control, so it never carries a name, a phone number or an identifier.
 */
export function buildAlert(signals: Signals, windowMinutes: number): Alert | null {
  const dead = Object.entries(signals.deadJobs)
    .filter(([, count]) => count > 0)
    .sort(([a], [b]) => a.localeCompare(b));
  const critical: string[] = [
    ...dead.map(([queue, count]) => `queue ${queue}: ${count} job(s) gave up`),
    ...(signals.failedMessages > 0 ? [`messages failed: ${signals.failedMessages}`] : []),
  ];
  const warning: string[] = [
    ...(signals.needsAttention > 0 ? [`sessions needing attention: ${signals.needsAttention}`] : []),
    ...(signals.inboundDeadLetters > 0 ? [`inbound payloads set aside: ${signals.inboundDeadLetters}`] : []),
  ];
  const lines = [...critical, ...warning];
  if (lines.length === 0) return null;
  return {
    severity: critical.length > 0 ? "critical" : "warning",
    title: `Kubri chatbot: ${lines.length} problem(s) in the last ${windowMinutes} min`,
    lines,
  };
}
```

```ts
  readonly url: string;
  /** Tells dev from prod in the same channel. */
  readonly label: string;

/** null = alerts are not configured. The address is a secret: errors name the variable only. */
export function parseAlertConfig(source: Readonly<Record<string, string | undefined>>): AlertConfig | null {
  const present = Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(source).filter(([, v]) => v !== undefined && v.trim() !== ""));
  const result = EnvSchema.safeParse(present);

    throw new Error(`Invalid alert configuration: ${result.error.issues.map((i) => i.path.join(".")).join(", ")}`);
  }
  return result.data.ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL ? { url: result.data.ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL, label: result.data.ALERT_LABEL } : null;
}

const TIMEOUT_MS = 10_000;

/** Slack and Mattermost read `text`, Discord reads `content`: both carry the same message. */
export function createWebhookAlertSink(url: string, label: string, fetchImpl: FetchLike = fetch): AlertSink {
  return {
    async send(alert) {
      const text = `[${alert.severity}] [${label}] ${alert.title}\n${alert.lines.join("\n")}`;
      const response = await fetchImpl(url, {
        method: "POST",
        headers: { "content-type": "application/json" },
        body: JSON.stringify({ text, content: text }),
        signal: AbortSignal.timeout(TIMEOUT_MS),
      });
      await response.text().catch(() => "");
      if (!response.ok) throw new Error(`alert webhook answered HTTP ${response.status}`);
    },
  };
}
```

```ts
  readonly logger: Logger;
  readonly sink: AlertSink;
  readonly queueSchema: string;

/**
 * D137. If the sink fails the job fails and is retried: an alert may then arrive twice, which is
 * better than never.
 */
export async function handleMonitor(deps: MonitorDeps): Promise<"quiet" | "alerted"> {
  const since = new Date(deps.now().getTime() - MONITOR_INTERVAL_MINUTES * 60_000);
  const signals = await collectSignals(deps.db, deps.queueSchema, since);
  const alert = buildAlert(signals, MONITOR_INTERVAL_MINUTES);

  deps.logger.warn({ severity: alert.severity, problems: alert.lines.length }, "monitor alert");
  await deps.sink.send(alert);
  return "alerted";
}
```

```ts
import { queueDepth, type QueueDepthRow } from "./queue-admin";

export interface SessionDescription {
  readonly id: string;
  readonly status: string;
  readonly phase: string | null;
  readonly consentsAccepted: boolean;
  readonly startedAt: Date;
  readonly lastActivityAt: Date;
  readonly messages: { readonly inbound: number; readonly outbound: number; readonly failed: number };
  readonly turns: readonly { readonly id: string; readonly outcome: string; readonly durationMs: number }[];
  readonly traces: readonly { readonly task: string; readonly count: number; readonly averageLatencyMs: number }[];
  readonly flags: readonly { readonly reason: string; readonly field: string | null; readonly needsAttention: boolean; readonly createdAt: Date }[];
  readonly profile: { readonly completed: boolean; readonly fieldsFilled: number; readonly cvDocuments: number } | null;
}

const phaseOf = (engineState: unknown): string | null =>
  typeof engineState === "object" && engineState !== null && "phase" in engineState && typeof engineState.phase === "string"
    ? engineState.phase
    : null;

const consentsOf = (engineState: unknown): boolean =>
  typeof engineState === "object" && engineState !== null && "consentsAccepted" in engineState && engineState.consentsAccepted === true;

/**
 * D138: what a person on call needs to understand a session. Statuses, counts and durations,
 * never a message, a profile value or a phone number.
 */
export async function describeSession(db: Database, sessionId: string): Promise<SessionDescription | null> {
  const session = await db.session.findUnique({
    where: { id: sessionId },
    include: {
      flags: { orderBy: { createdAt: "asc" } },
      turns: { orderBy: { startedAt: "asc" }, select: { id: true, outcome: true, startedAt: true, completedAt: true } },
      profile: { select: { completedAt: true, standard: true, custom: true, _count: { select: { cvDocuments: true } } } },
    },
  });
  if (!session) return null;
  const [byDirection, failed, traces] = await Promise.all([
    db.message.groupBy({ by: ["direction"], where: { sessionId }, _count: { _all: true } }),
    db.message.count({ where: { sessionId, status: "failed" } }),
    db.nluTrace.groupBy({ by: ["task"], where: { turn: { sessionId } }, _count: { _all: true }, _avg: { latencyMs: true } }),
  ]);
  const count = (direction: string) => byDirection.find((row) => row.direction === direction)?._count._all ?? 0;
  const filled = (value: unknown) => (typeof value === "object" && value !== null ? Object.keys(value).length : 0);
  return {
    id: session.id,
    status: session.status,
    phase: phaseOf(session.engineState),
    consentsAccepted: consentsOf(session.engineState),
    startedAt: session.startedAt,
    lastActivityAt: session.lastActivityAt,
    messages: { inbound: count("inbound"), outbound: count("outbound"), failed },
    turns: session.turns.map((t) => ({ id: t.id, outcome: t.outcome, durationMs: t.completedAt.getTime() - t.startedAt.getTime() })),
    traces: traces.map((t) => ({ task: t.task, count: t._count._all, averageLatencyMs: Math.round(t._avg.latencyMs ?? 0) })),
    flags: session.flags.map((f) => ({ reason: f.reason, field: f.field, needsAttention: f.needsAttention, createdAt: f.createdAt })),
    profile: session.profile
      ? {
          completed: session.profile.completedAt !== null,
          fieldsFilled: filled(session.profile.standard) + filled(session.profile.custom),
          cvDocuments: session.profile._count.cvDocuments,
        }
      : null,
  };
}

export interface Stats {
  readonly sessions: Readonly<Record<string, number>>;
  readonly queues: readonly QueueDepthRow[];
  readonly failedMessages24h: number;
  readonly averageTurnMs24h: number | null;
}

export async function collectStats(db: Database, queueSchema: string, now: Date): Promise<Stats> {
  assertSchemaName(queueSchema);
  const since = new Date(now.getTime() - 24 * 3600_000);
  const [sessions, queues, failedMessages24h, turns] = await Promise.all([
    db.session.groupBy({ by: ["status"], _count: { _all: true } }),
    queueDepth(db, queueSchema),
    db.message.count({ where: { status: "failed", failedAt: { gte: since } } }),
    db.turn.findMany({ where: { startedAt: { gte: since } }, select: { startedAt: true, completedAt: true } }),
  ]);
  const total = turns.reduce((sum, t) => sum + (t.completedAt.getTime() - t.startedAt.getTime()), 0);
  return {
    sessions: Object.fromEntries(sessions.map((row) => [row.status, row._count._all])),
    queues,
    failedMessages24h,
    averageTurnMs24h: turns.length === 0 ? null : Math.round(total / turns.length),
  };
}
```

```ts
import type { Stats } from "../store/ops";

/** Plain text columns for a terminal. */
export function formatTable(header: readonly string[], rows: readonly (readonly string[])[]): string {
  if (rows.length === 0) return "(none)";
  const widths = header.map((h, i) => Math.max(h.length, ...rows.map((r) => (r[i] ?? "").length)));
  const line = (cells: readonly string[]) => cells.map((c, i) => c.padEnd(widths[i] ?? 0)).join("  ").trimEnd();
  return [line(header), ...rows.map(line)].join("\n");
}

export function formatStats(stats: Stats): string {
  const sessions = Object.entries(stats.sessions).map(([status, n]) => [status, String(n)] as const);
  const queues = stats.queues.map((q) => [q.name, q.state, String(q.n)] as const);
  const turn = stats.averageTurnMs24h === null ? "n/a" : `${(stats.averageTurnMs24h / 1000).toFixed(1)} s`;
  return [
    "Sessions",
    formatTable(["status", "n"], sessions.map(([a, b]) => [a.padEnd(8), b])),
    "",
    "Queues",
    formatTable(["queue", "state", "n"], queues),
    "",
    `messages failed (24h): ${stats.failedMessages24h}`,
    `average turn (24h): ${turn}`,
  ].join("\n");
}
```

```ts
        if (!options.yes) throw new CliError(`${action} changes the queue: repeat with --yes`);
        const env = parseEnv(process.env);
        const config = parseRuntimeConfig(process.env);
        const runtime = createQueueRuntime({ connectionString: toPgConnectionString(env.DATABASE_URL), schema: config.queueSchema });
        await runtime.start();
        try {
          if (action === "retry") {
            deps.out((await runtime.retryDead(queue, id)) ? "queued again" : "no such dead job");
          } else {
            await runtime.discardDead(queue, id);
            deps.out("discarded");
          }
        } finally {
          await runtime.stop();
        }
      });
  }
}
```

```ts
import { createServer } from "node:http";
import type { AddressInfo } from "node:net";

export interface HealthServer {
  readonly port: number;
  close(): Promise<void>;
}

/**
 * D139: the platform asks "is the worker alive?" and gets 200 or 503. `check` is the worker's
 * own idea of alive (the database answers, the queue is started). Nothing else is served.
 */
export function startHealthServer(options: {
  readonly port: number;
  readonly check: () => Promise<boolean>;
}): Promise<HealthServer> {
  const server = createServer((request, response) => {
    if (request.method !== "GET" || request.url !== "/health") {
      response.writeHead(404).end();
      return;
    }
    options
      .check()
      .catch(() => false)
      .then((ok) => response.writeHead(ok ? 200 : 503).end(ok ? "ok" : "unavailable"))
      .catch(() => undefined);
  });
  return new Promise((resolve, reject) => {
    server.once("error", reject);
    server.listen(options.port, () => {
      resolve({
        port: (server.address() as AddressInfo).port,
        close: () => new Promise<void>((done) => server.close(() => done())),
      });
    });
  });
}
```

## Punti aperti

1. **Durate di conservazione.** I valori di D132 sono provvisori. Le decide il titolare del
   trattamento (spec §18.6): quanto restano i messaggi, le tracce, i file; quanto una sessione
   abbandonata prima e dopo la soglia dei consensi. Il meccanismo è pronto, mancano le cifre.
2. **Testi dei consensi e titolare per ogni campagna** (§18.5). Finché sono segnaposto, la campagna
   non si importa in produzione: il test d'integrazione lo mostra (con un `skipIf` da togliere).
3. **Monitoraggio degli errori con regione UE** (§13.1). Gli avvisi di D137 coprono i guasti
   *noti*; per le eccezioni non previste serve un servizio (tipo Sentry in regione UE, o
   equivalente europeo) da scegliere con l'hosting, scrubbing dei dati personali incluso.
4. **Fornitore di hosting** (§18.12) e **provider LLM definitivo** dopo gli evals. Le guide
   hanno il punto dove inserire i comandi del fornitore scelto.
5. **Follow-up delle sessioni abbandonate** (§17, §18.7): oggi `abandoned` conserva soltanto i
   dati; nessun messaggio parte. Servono template Meta approvati.
6. **Scenari Make collegati** (follow-up, Telegram, §18.4): non sono sostituiti.
7. **Immagine più piccola.** Contiene le dipendenze di sviluppo (D140); si può ridurre con un
   build a due stadi quando si potrà provare la potatura del lockfile.
8. **Avvisi senza stato** (D137): un giro del `monitor` saltato è un fatto non segnalato. Se
   diventa un problema, memorizzare l'ultimo istante controllato in una tabella.
9. **Richieste di cancellazione che arrivano durante un turno.** `delete-contact` cancella il
   contatto anche se un job `process-contact` è in corso: il job in corso fallisce al commit e
   viene ritentato, trovando il contatto sparito. Verificare con una prova a mano su dev.
10. **Parole del candidato nei backup di Supabase.** La cancellazione non tocca i backup del
    fornitore: va scritta nell'informativa e nei tempi di conservazione dei backup.
11. **Più istanze del worker.** I job pianificati sono accodati una sola volta da pg-boss
    (non per istanza), ma la correttezza con più worker va misurata sotto carico (piano 08, punto
    sulla concorrenza).
