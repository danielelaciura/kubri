# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences with very different contexts:

- **Operators** (primary surface: the dashboard). Staff of Italian social cooperatives, employment agencies (APL) and public employment centres. They work on laptops or tablets, screening and matching entry-level candidates (waiters, warehouse workers, cleaners, logistics, kitchen staff) against job descriptions. Job to be done: find the right people for an open role quickly, and keep track of them.
- **Candidates** (public surfaces: the `/candidati/[slug]` landing, the WhatsApp chatbot). People looking for entry-level work, often with language barriers or informal work experience. They arrive on a phone, from a printed QR code or a shared link, frequently on a slow connection. Job to be done: apply to an organization in a few minutes, either by chatting on WhatsApp or by uploading a CV, without needing an account.

## Product Purpose

Kubri AI runs multilingual screening interviews on WhatsApp/Telegram and turns the answers (or an uploaded CV) into structured candidate profiles. The dashboard lets client organizations view, filter, match and manage those profiles. Kubri S.r.l. is an Italian Società Benefit: success means more people from vulnerable backgrounds reach a real job interview, and operators spend less time on manual screening.

## Brand Personality

Modern, positive, energetic. Speaks to candidates as capable adults looking for work: direct, warm, encouraging, never bureaucratic and never childish. To operators: efficient and trustworthy, the tool stays out of the way.

## Anti-references

- Public-administration or bureaucratic portals: long forms, dense text, institutional look (regional job portals, "sportello" websites).
- Childish or patronising apps: over-playful illustrations, cartoon tone, talking down to adults in a job search.

## Design Principles

1. **One clear next step.** Every public screen has a single obvious action; secondary paths are visible but quiet.
2. **Phone first for candidates, desk first for operators.** Public pages are designed at 375px on a slow network; the dashboard for laptops and tablets.
3. **Plain words, any language.** Short sentences, no HR jargon, IT/EN parity; the copy must survive being read by someone learning Italian.
4. **Show progress, never leave people guessing.** Uploads, checks and waits always say what is happening and what comes next.
5. **Dignity and privacy by default.** Ask only what is needed, explain why, keep personal data out of sight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, full keyboard and screen-reader support, touch targets ≥ 44px on public pages, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Copy at roughly B1 Italian; never rely on colour alone to convey state.
